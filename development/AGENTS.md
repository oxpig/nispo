# Development-only nomenclature acceptance

This ledger is a development artifact. Previously granted permissions to use
panel-derived cases remain in force, with their original provenance. Do not add
other panel molecules, reference names, individual failures or derived results
without further user authorization. The complete panel may be used for frozen
aggregate evaluation.

Use explicitly authorized primary cases, independent published examples or
independently chosen graph controls elsewhere; record the origin. Do not store
complete expected PIN strings elsewhere or put nomenclature expectations in
pytest.

Resolved obligations are release gates. Pending obligations remain visible
failures and are not passes. Resolving one local obligation does not certify a
whole name as a PIN. Assess newly passing pending obligations before changing
their state. Keep wording/ranking checks here, outside pytest; pytest protects
public API, graph identity, batch determinism and telemetry contracts.
